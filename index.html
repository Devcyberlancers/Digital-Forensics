YOUR INVESTIGATION GUIDE

How the machines work

Each machine is a separate forensic case with its own WebApp and evidence. You only need the files for the case you choose.

  1. Choose a case. Search the directory by machine number, name or topic, then select Open investigation. Read the case briefing and questions.
  2. Download the evidence. Use the case's download button and extract its ZIP to your computer. A separate virtual-machine download is not required for the portal.
  3. Prepare your tools. Check that machine's tools guide and use the relevant tools, such as Autopsy or Wireshark. Tool availability and installation depend on your system.
  4. Preserve and investigate. Keep the original evidence unchanged, verify its hash when the case asks, and examine the artifacts to derive each finding.
  5. Submit and track. Enter answers in that machine's WebApp using its stated format. Progress may be stored in your current browser; clearing its data or resetting a case can remove it.

Walkthroughs: The walkthrough tab is intended for authorized instructors and may require a password. Do not share instructor answers with students during an investigation.
